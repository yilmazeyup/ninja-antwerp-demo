# Photos

All photos in this folder are Ninja Antwerp's own, taken from its website (ninja-antwerp.be) in October 2026:

- `kebab-platter`, `durum`, `iskender`, `soup`, `platter-rice`, `ninja-rolls`, `yoghurt-dip`, `carrot-dip`, `hummus`,
  `ezme`, `shepherd-salad`, `falafel-hummus`, `lavash`, `fries`, `turkish-rice`, `rice-pudding` and the `menu-*` photos:
  cut from Ninja's menu (NinjaMenu.pdf, June 2026).
- `room-*`: the large photos on the site's pages (home, menu, FAQ, reservations, jobs).
- `gal-*` and `flash-*`: the site's gallery and home slider (the test night of 23 January 2024 and later evenings).

They are used here only to show Ninja its own demo. The logo files in `../brand` are Ninja's own as well.

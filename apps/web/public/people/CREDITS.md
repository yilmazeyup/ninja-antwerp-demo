# Match portraits (web copies)

Sixteen stock portraits for the invented guests of Ninja Match (`PERSONAS` in `packages/shared/src/social.ts`), and one (`me`) for the demo’s own guest. None of
them is a real guest of Ninja. All are free photos from Unsplash under the Unsplash License
(https://unsplash.com/license): free to use, also commercially, without asking. Each page names the photographer.
Cropped to 900 × 1100 around the face. `apps/web/public/people/*.webp` are the same pictures for the panel and the
website.
